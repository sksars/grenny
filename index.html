Granny ka Ghar

Ek waqt mein ek hi item haath mein. E = uthao / use karo, Q = rakho, G = fenko. Chamakte furniture par sahi item lagao to andar se agla item milta hai. Garage ke liye Master Key, gasoline wale room ke liye Padlock Key, upar ke room ke liye Rusty Key. Car mein 7 parts lagao (Car Key, Battery, Spark Plug, Gasoline, Wheel Crank, Engine Oil, Fan Belt) aur bhaag jao. Chhat (terrace) par jaane ke liye upar wale floor ki doosri stairs lo — wahan 2 naye task aur items hain. Sirf 5 din milte hain: Granny pakde to naya din bed se shuru hota hai, 5 din khatam to game over. V / Left click se haath ke item se Granny par attack karo (Hammer aur Pipe Wrench sabse zyada stun karte hain). Melon fenko to wo phat kar Granny ko door bula leta hai.
